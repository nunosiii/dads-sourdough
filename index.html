Recipe

74.5% hydration, long cold proof for blisters, tight shape for height. KitchenAid 6qt with the dough hook, never above speed 2.

Keep screen on while bakingStops your phone or tablet from sleeping while this page is open.

Ingredients

4 loaves won't fit in one 6qt bowl. Mix two batches of 2 loaves about 15 minutes apart. Amounts below are the total for all 4; the method shows per-batch amounts.

Whole wheat is optional. To skip it, use all bread flour (500g per loaf) and hold back an extra 5–10g water at first. Expect a milder flavor, a slightly slower bulk, and possibly a touch more height.
